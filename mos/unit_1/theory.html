Unit 1 — Strength of Materials

This unit develops the fundamental understanding of how engineering materials respond to mechanical and thermal loading. Students will learn how external loads produce internal stress and deformation, how stress and strain are related through material properties, and how different materials behave under loading.

The unit also introduces the analysis of axially loaded members, deformation due to self-weight and temperature, and the energy stored during elastic deformation.

  • Understand the relationship between load, stress, strain and deformation.
  • Understand elastic, plastic, ductile and brittle behaviour.
  • Study elastic moduli and their relationships.
  • Understand stepped and composite bars.
  • Understand deformation due to self-weight and temperature.
  • Understand strain energy and resilience.
