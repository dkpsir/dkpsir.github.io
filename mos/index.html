About the Course

Mechanics of Solids is a fundamental course that deals with the behaviour of solid materials subjected to different types of loading. The course develops an understanding of stress and strain and their relationship with the deformation and strength of engineering members. It covers the analysis of shear force and bending moment, bending and shear stresses, deflections of beams, torsion of shafts, columns and struts, and stresses in thin and thick cylinders. The concepts are supported through numerical problems and important questions to help students understand the theory and apply it to engineering situations.
