The derivation of the standard torsion equation \( \left( \frac{T}{J} = \frac{\tau}{R} = \frac{G\theta}{L} \right) \) relies on the following fundamental assumptions[cite: 9]:

  • Material Properties: The material of the shaft is homogeneous, isotropic, and perfectly elastic (i.e., it obeys Hooke’s Law, and all stresses remain within the elastic limit)[cite: 9].
  • Cross-sectional Uniformity: The shaft is initially perfectly straight, and its cross-section is uniform and strictly circular throughout its entire length[cite: 9].
  • Plane Sections Remain Plane: Cross-sections of the shaft that are plane before twisting remain plane and parallel after twisting (there is no axial warping or distortion)[cite: 9].
  • Radial Lines: Radii that are straight before the application of torque remain straight after the twist is applied[cite: 9].
  • Shear Stress Distribution: The shear stress induced is proportional to the distance from the central axis. It is zero at the geometric center and reaches a maximum value at the outermost surface of the shaft[cite: 9].
Given Data:
\[ \begin{aligned} D &= 400\ \text{mm}\\ \tau &= 40\ \text{N/mm}^2\\ L &= 10\ \text{m} = 10,000\ \text{mm}\\ G &= 80\ \text{kN/mm}^2 = 80,000\ \text{N/mm}^2\\ \theta &= 2^\circ = 2 \times \left(\frac{\pi}{180}\right) = 0.03491\ \text{rad} \end{aligned} \]
a) Based on Strength (Shear Stress)
Maximum Torque, \(T_1\)
\[ \begin{aligned} T_1 &= \frac{\pi}{16} \tau D^3\\ &= \frac{\pi}{16} \times 40 \times (400)^3\\ &= 502,654,824.57\ \text{N}\cdot\text{mm} \end{aligned} \]
\(T_1\) = 502.65 kN·m
b) Based on Stiffness (Angle of Twist)
Polar Moment of Inertia, \(J\)
\[J = \frac{\pi}{32} D^4 = \frac{\pi}{32} (400)^4 = 2.513 \times 10^9\ \text{mm}^4\]
Maximum Torque, \(T_2\)
\[ \begin{aligned} T_2 &= \frac{G \cdot \theta \cdot J}{L}\\ &= \frac{80,000 \times 0.03491 \times 2.513 \times 10^9}{10,000}\\ &= 701,838,535.19\ \text{N}\cdot\text{mm} \end{aligned} \]
\(T_2\) = 701.84 kN·m
Final Selection:

To operate safely without exceeding the allowable shear stress limit AND without twisting more than the allowable angle, the shaft must be subjected to the lower of the two calculated torques.

Safe Transmitted Torque = 502.65 kN·m
Given Data:
\[ \begin{aligned} P &= 100\ \text{kW} = 100,000\ \text{W}\\ N &= 160\ \text{rpm}\\ \tau &= 60\ \text{N/mm}^2\\ L &= 3000\ \text{mm}\\ \theta &= 1^\circ = 1 \times \left(\frac{\pi}{180}\right) = 0.01745\ \text{rad}\\ G &= 80,000\ \text{N/mm}^2 \end{aligned} \]
1. Calculate Transmitted Torque (T)
\[ \begin{aligned} P &= \frac{2 \pi N T}{60} \implies T = \frac{60 P}{2 \pi N}\\ T &= \frac{60 \times 100,000}{2 \pi \times 160} = 5968.31\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}\\ T &= 5,968,310.36\ \text{N}\cdot\text{mm} \end{aligned} \]
2. Design Based on Strength
\[ \begin{aligned} T &= \frac{\pi}{16} \tau D^3\\ 5,968,310.36 &= \frac{\pi}{16} \times 60 \times D^3\\ D^3 &= 506,604.73 \end{aligned} \]
\(D\) = 79.72 mm
3. Design Based on Stiffness
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{T}{J} &= \frac{G \theta}{L} \implies \frac{T}{\frac{\pi}{32} D^4} = \frac{G \theta}{L}\\ \frac{5,968,310.36 \times 32}{\pi \times D^4} &= \frac{80,000 \times 0.01745}{3000}\\ D^4 &= 130,653,399.5 \end{aligned} \]
\(D\) = 106.91 mm
Final Selection:

To satisfy both the strength and stiffness criteria without failing, the larger of the two calculated diameters must be selected.

Suitable Shaft Diameter (D) = 107 mm
Given Data:
\[ \begin{aligned} P &= 6000\ \text{W}, \quad N = 200\ \text{rpm}\\ \tau &= 90\ \text{MPa} = 90\ \text{N/mm}^2\\ G &= 85\ \text{GPa} = 85,000\ \text{N/mm}^2\\ \theta &= 3^\circ\text{/m} = \left(3 \times \frac{\pi}{180}\right) = 0.05236\ \text{rad/m}\\ L &= 1\ \text{m} = 1000\ \text{mm} \end{aligned} \]
Transmitted Torque (T)
\[T = \frac{60 \times 6000}{2 \pi \times 200} = 286.48\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m} = 286,478.9\ \text{N}\cdot\text{mm}\]
(i) Design of Solid Shaft (\(D_s\))

Based on Strength:

\[ \begin{aligned} T &= \frac{\pi}{16} \tau D_s^3\\ 286,478.9 &= \frac{\pi}{16} \times 90 \times D_s^3 \end{aligned} \]
\(D_s\) (Strength) = 25.31 mm

Based on Stiffness:

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{T}{J} &= \frac{G \theta}{L} \implies \frac{\pi}{32} D_s^4 = \frac{T \cdot L}{G \cdot \theta}\\ D_s^4 &= \frac{286,478.9 \times 1000 \times 32}{\pi \times 85,000 \times 0.05236} \end{aligned} \]
\(D_s\) (Stiffness) = 28.46 mm
Select Solid \(D_s\) = 28.5 mm
(ii) Design of Hollow Shaft (\(D, d\))
Hollow Shaft Parameters:
\[\frac{D}{d} = 1.2 \implies d = \frac{D}{1.2} = 0.8333D\]
\[r = \frac{D}{2}\]
\[ \begin{aligned} J &= \frac{\pi}{32} (D^4 - d^4)\\ &= \frac{\pi}{32} (D^4 - (0.8333D)^4)\\ &= \frac{\pi}{32} (0.5177 D^4) = 0.0508 D^4 \end{aligned} \]

Based on Strength:

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{T}{J} &= \frac{\tau}{r}\\ \frac{286,478.9}{0.0508 D^4} &= \frac{90}{(D/2)}\\ \frac{286,478.9}{0.0508} &= 180 D^3 \end{aligned} \]
\(D\) (Strength) = 31.52 mm

Based on Stiffness:

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{T}{J} &= \frac{G \theta}{L}\\ \frac{286,478.9}{0.0508 D^4} &= \frac{85,000 \times 0.05236}{1000}\\ D^4 &= \frac{286,478.9}{0.0508 \times 4.4506} \end{aligned} \]
\(D\) (Stiffness) = 33.55 mm
Select Hollow \(D\) = 33.55 mm
\(d\) = 33.55 / 1.2 = 27.96 mm
Given Data:
\[ \begin{aligned} P &= 300\ \text{kW} = 300,000\ \text{W}\\ N &= 200\ \text{rpm}\\ t &= 20\ \text{mm}\\ \tau &= 3\ \text{N/mm}^2 \end{aligned} \]
Note: Modulus of rigidity (G) is extraneous here since no limiting angle of twist is provided.
1. Calculate Torque Transmitted (T)
\[ \begin{aligned} T &= \frac{60 P}{2 \pi N} = \frac{60 \times 300,000}{2 \pi \times 200}\\ T &= 14,323.94\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m} = 14,323,944.88\ \text{N}\cdot\text{mm} \end{aligned} \]
2. Define Hollow Shaft Parameters (\(r, J\))

Let \(D\) be the external diameter. The internal diameter \(d\) is \(d = D - 2t = D - 40\).

\[r = \frac{D}{2}\]
\[J = \frac{\pi}{32} (D^4 - d^4) = \frac{\pi}{32} (D^4 - (D - 40)^4)\]
3. Relate Diameters using the Basic Torsion Equation
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{T}{J} &= \frac{\tau}{r}\\ \frac{14,323,944.88}{\frac{\pi}{32} (D^4 - (D - 40)^4)} &= \frac{3}{(D/2)}\\ 14,323,944.88 \left( \frac{D}{2} \right) &= 3 \left( \frac{\pi}{32} \right) (D^4 - (D - 40)^4)\\ 7,161,972.44 D &= 0.29452 (D^4 - (D - 40)^4) \end{aligned} \]
4. Solve for External Diameter (D)

Expand the binomial term \((D - 40)^4\):

\[(D - 40)^4 = D^4 - 160D^3 + 9600D^2 - 256,000D + 2,560,000\]

Substitute this back to set up the polynomial:

\[ \begin{aligned} 7,161,972.44 D &= 0.29452 (160D^3 - 9600D^2 + 256,000D - 2,560,000)\\ 24,317,074 D &= 160D^3 - 9600D^2 + 256,000D - 2,560,000\\ 160D^3 - 9600D^2 - 24,061,074 D - 2,560,000 &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

Divide by 160 to simplify the cubic equation:

\[D^3 - 60D^2 - 150,381.71D - 16,000 = 0\]

Solving this cubic equation yields one valid positive real root:

External Diameter (D) ≈ 419 mm
