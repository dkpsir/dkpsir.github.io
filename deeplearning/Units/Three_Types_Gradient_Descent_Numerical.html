Important Teaching Point

The final MSE values should not be interpreted as saying that SGD is always better than Batch GD or Mini-Batch GD.

The values above are obtained after one update pass through this small dataset, using the same learning rate. They are not a comparison of the methods after full convergence.

With sufficient iterations and suitable hyperparameters, all three methods can progressively reduce the loss toward a good solution.
