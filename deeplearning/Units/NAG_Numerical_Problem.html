NAG Equations Used

Physical meaning:
NAG uses the momentum from the previous step to look ahead before calculating the gradient. Therefore, the gradient is evaluated at a future-looking position rather than simply at the current parameter position. This allows NAG to anticipate the direction of movement and can produce faster and smoother convergence than ordinary momentum-based updates.
Key teaching point:
The most important difference to remember is:

Momentum: calculate the gradient at the current position, then use momentum.
NAG: first look ahead using momentum, then calculate the gradient at that look-ahead position.
