Learning Materials

UNIT I - Basics of Neural Networks

This unit introduces the fundamental concepts of Artificial Neural Networks and Deep Learning. Students will learn:

  • Biological and Artificial Neurons
  • McCulloch–Pitts Neuron Model
  • Linear Perceptron and Learning Rule
  • Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)
  • Activation Functions and their Applications
📘 Learning Material of Unit 1

UNIT II - Better Training of Neural Networks

This unit focuses on improving the training and generalization of deep neural networks. Students will learn:

  • Risk Minimization and Loss Functions
  • Gradient Descent and Backpropagation
  • Optimization Algorithms
  • Regularization Techniques
  • Batch Normalization and Dropout
📘 Learning Material of Unit 2

UNIT III - Convolutional Neural Networks

This unit explains deep learning techniques for image processing and computer vision. Students will learn:

  • CNN Architecture
  • Convolution and Pooling Layers
  • Feature Maps
  • Popular CNN Models
  • Image Classification
📘 Learning Material of Unit 3

UNIT IV - Recurrent Neural Networks

This unit introduces neural networks designed for sequential and time-series data. Students will learn:

  • Sequential Data Processing
  • Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
  • LSTM Networks
  • GRU Networks
  • Bidirectional RNN
📘 Learning Material of Unit 4

UNIT V - Recent Trends in Deep Learning

This unit explores modern deep learning architectures and emerging research areas. Students will learn:

  • Autoencoders
  • Transformers and Attention Mechanism
  • Energy-Based Models
  • Boltzmann Machines
  • Recent Deep Learning Applications
