Batch Normalisation — Complete Numerical Problem

Problem Statement

Consider the outputs of a neuron for a mini-batch of 5 samples:
x = [2, 4, 6, 8, 10]
Calculate the Batch Normalisation output for each sample and finally verify that the mean after normalisation is 0 and the variance is 1.

Assume:
γ = 1,    β = 0,    ε = 0

μB = (1/m) Σxi
μB = (2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10) / 5
μB = 6

Physical meaning:
Batch Normalisation centres the activations of the current mini-batch around zero and scales their spread so that the normalised activations have unit variance. The learnable parameters γ and β can later scale and shift this normalised output when required by the network.
